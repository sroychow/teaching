Partial differential equations by separation of variables

This module supports Unit 3: PDE solutions using separation of variables, with emphasis on heat/diffusion-type equations and mode decay.

PDESeparation of variablesHeat equation

Concepts explained

  • A heat equation evolves an initial temperature profile with time.
  • Separation of variables breaks the solution into spatial modes and time-decay factors.
  • Higher modes decay faster because their decay factor contains n².
  • The graph shows how the temperature profile smooths out with increasing time.
