Probability theory and error analysis

This module supports Unit 4: random variables, binomial/Poisson/normal distributions, central limit theorem, systematic/random errors, propagation of errors, standard error, and probable error.

ProbabilityNormal distributionError analysis

Concepts explained

  • Repeated measurements fluctuate due to random error.
  • The mean estimates the central value of the measurement.
  • The standard deviation measures spread; standard error measures uncertainty in the mean.
  • Probable error is approximately 0.6745 times the standard error.
  • Large samples often approach a normal distribution by the central limit theorem.
standard error = s/√N, probable error ≈ 0.6745 s/√N
