Topic covered

Beta and Gamma functions

This module supports Unit 2: special integrals, the relation between Beta and Gamma functions, and expression of integrals in terms of Gamma functions.

Gamma functionBeta functionNumerical integration

Concepts explained

  • Γ(a) generalizes factorials: Γ(n) = (n−1)! for positive integers.
  • B(p,q) measures an integral over the interval [0,1].
  • The relation B(p,q)=Γ(p)Γ(q)/Γ(p+q) connects the two.
  • Numerical integration helps students see how area under a curve becomes a special function.
Γ(a)=∫₀∞ x^(a−1)e^(−x)dx B(p,q)=∫₀¹ x^(p−1)(1−x)^(q−1)dx
