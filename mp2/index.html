Syllabus modules

Each module is shown with the core topics to be supported by tutorials, simulators, assignments and AI prompts.

Unit 1

Frobenius Method and Special Functions

25 lecture hours
  • Singular points of second-order linear differential equations.
  • Frobenius method and series solutions.
  • Legendre, Bessel, Hermite and Laguerre differential equations.
  • Rodrigues formula, generating functions, orthogonality and recurrence relations.
  • Zeros of Bessel functions and expansion in Legendre polynomials.
Unit 2

Some Special Integrals

10 lecture hours
  • Beta and Gamma functions.
  • Relation between Beta and Gamma functions.
  • Conversion of definite integrals into Gamma-function form.
  • Numerical comparison of integral values with analytical expressions.
Unit 3

Partial Differential Equations

15 lecture hours
  • Separation of variables.
  • Laplace equation in rectangular, cylindrical and spherical symmetry.
  • Wave equation and vibrational modes of a stretched string.
  • Rectangular and circular membrane modes.
Unit 4

Probability Theory and Error Analysis

10 lecture hours
  • Random variables and probability distributions.
  • Binomial, Poisson and normal distributions.
  • Central limit theorem and error function.
  • Systematic/random errors, propagation of errors, standard and probable error.
