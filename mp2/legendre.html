Topic covered

Legendre polynomials and special functions

This module supports Unit 1: Frobenius method and special functions, especially Legendre differential equation, generating functions, recurrence relations, orthogonality, and expansion of functions in Legendre series.

Special functionsOrthogonalitySeries modes

Concepts explained

  • Legendre polynomials Pₙ(x) appear as solutions of Legendre's differential equation.
  • Different n values correspond to different angular modes.
  • Orthogonality means ∫₋₁¹ Pₘ(x)Pₙ(x)dx = 0 for m ≠ n.
  • Recurrence relation: (n+1)Pₙ₊₁(x) = (2n+1)xPₙ(x) − nPₙ₋₁(x).
