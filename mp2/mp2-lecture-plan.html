Course Outcomes

CO1

Understand the essential mathematical basics required for solving problems in Physics.

CO2

Perform error analysis and presentation of results of an experiment.

CO3

Compute and comprehend the required differential and integral calculus tools using logical steps.

CO4

Analyze the solution of partial differential equations to solve physics problems of moderate complexity.

Syllabus Block

Unit I: Frobenius Method and Special Functions

27 Lecture Hours

Singular points of second-order linear differential equations, Frobenius method, Legendre, Bessel, Hermite and Laguerre equations, recurrence relations, generating functions, orthogonality and expansions.

Unit II: Some Special Integrals

6 Lecture Hours

Beta and Gamma functions, relation between them, and expression of definite integrals in terms of Gamma functions.

Unit III: Partial Differential Equations

15 Lecture Hours

Separation of variables; Laplace equation in rectangular, cylindrical and spherical symmetry; wave equation and vibrational modes of strings and membranes.

Unit IV: Probability Theory and Error Analysis

12 Lecture Hours

Probability theory, random variables, binomial, Poisson and normal distributions, central limit theorem, error function, systematic/random errors, propagation of errors and normal law of errors.
