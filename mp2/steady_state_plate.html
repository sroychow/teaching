Steady-State Temperature Distribution in a Semi-Infinite Rectangular Plate

A two-dimensional Laplace equation simulator for a rectangular plate of width L, extending infinitely in the positive y-direction. The base y = 0 is held at a user-defined temperature function f(x), while the side boundaries and the far end are maintained at zero temperature.

Student workflow

Enter a base temperature function, choose plate width, visualize the heat map, export the computed grid as CSV, and reflect on Fourier sine modes.

Concepts explained

Since the system is in steady state, temperature does not change with time. The temperature satisfies Laplace’s equation:

∂²T/∂x² + ∂²T/∂y² = 0

Boundary conditions:

T(x,0)=f(x),   T(0,y)=0,   T(L,y)=0,   T(x,y)→0 as y→∞

The separation-of-variables solution is:

T(x,y)=Σ bₙ sin(nπx/L) exp(-nπy/L),   bₙ=(2/L)∫₀ᴸ f(x)sin(nπx/L)dx
